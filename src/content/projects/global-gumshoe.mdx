## What it is

A modern, all-original tribute to the geography-detective games I grew up with. You get a case, a five-to-seven city trail, and a seven-day deadline. Interview witnesses at the embassy, library, and bank to collect geographic and suspect clues, narrow the dossier to one villain, issue a warrant, and arrive in the right city to make the arrest.

## How it works

- **Procedural cases.** A generator builds the trail, places clues (geographic plus suspect features), and guarantees exactly one matching villain, so a case is always solvable once the clues are gathered.
- **Pure-functional engine.** Case generation, clue matching, and arrest logic are deterministic and live separately from the React UI. State stays in React, no extra store.
- **Proven solvable.** A headless smoke test plays randomized cases across all four regions and three difficulty tiers, asserting trail validity and clue logic across thousands of checks.
- **Light and fast.** React, TypeScript, and Vite compiled to a 65 KB gzipped bundle, responsive down to an iPhone home-screen PWA.

## What's real about it

Every character and the lore are original; the mechanics are the tribute. Single-player, local save, live and playable. The interesting part is that the engine proves its own fairness on every build.
