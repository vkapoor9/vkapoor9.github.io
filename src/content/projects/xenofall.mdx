## What it is

A Doom-style alien shooter that runs in a browser tab. Three.js does the 3D (vendored, no build step, works offline). You fight through seven story missions aboard a colony ship, each with its own biome, lighting, and fog, up to a boss arena against the Hive Queen.

## How it works

- **Real-time 3D.** An EffectComposer pipeline with bloom and ACES tone mapping; per-level themes drive palette, fog, and lighting across seven distinct looks.
- **Real movement.** Gravity, double-jump, jump pads, and damaging pits. The verticality is actual math in the logic layer, not a visual trick.
- **Everything synthesized.** Weapon fire, impacts, enemy snarls, explosions, and the adaptive music are all generated live with WebAudio oscillators and filters. Wall textures are drawn procedurally on a canvas. No mp3, no wav, no image files.
- **Tested first.** Geometry, collision, damage, objectives, save/load, and all the enemy AI types are covered by 194 unit assertions, with a Playwright headless run exercising the full mission, boss, death, and pause paths.

## What's real about it

Single-player, client-side save, live and playable. The point of it is the engineering: a tested 3D game with no external dependencies at runtime.
